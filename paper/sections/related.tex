\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Energy-based reasoners.} EBT owns scalar-energy confidence and adaptive compute
\citep{ebt2025}, and IREM/IRED own the learned inference-time landscape \citep{irem2022,ired2024}, but
none use \emph{restarts}, \emph{landscape geometry}, or a \emph{calibration guarantee}. We add all
three on top of such a reasoner, and show---adversarially---that the geometry advantage over the
scalar energy does not extend to beating a softmax confidence under our current (basin-center)
reasoner.

\paragraph{Curvature and calibration.} Work relating sharpness/curvature to calibration and
generalization operates over the loss in \emph{weight} space \citep{sam2020,toosharp2026}; our
curvature is of the energy over the \emph{latent}, per input, computed at inference---a different
quantity with a different purpose (a per-input reliability feature, not a training regularizer).

\paragraph{Uncertainty and abstention.} MSP + temperature scaling \citep{tempscaling2017}, deep
ensembles \citep{deepensembles2017}, MC-dropout \citep{mcdropout2016}, and energy-based OOD detection
\citep{energyood2020} are static, scalar confidence signals; SelectiveNet learns a reject option
end-to-end \citep{selectivenet2019}. We instead extract signal from the \emph{dynamics of inference}
and wrap it in a distribution-free certificate (LTT/CRC, \citep{ltt2021,crc2022}), with an explicit,
demonstrated failure mode under distribution shift.

\paragraph{Adaptive computation.} PonderNet and related methods learn halting policies
\citep{pondernet2021}; EBT ``thinks longer'' on hard inputs via energy \citep{ebt2025}. Our halting
carries a \emph{finite-sample error budget} via CRC rather than a learned or heuristic stop rule. To
our knowledge, no prior work turns inference-time landscape geometry into a distribution-free
selective certificate for an EBM reasoner.
