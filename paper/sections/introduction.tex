\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

An energy-based reasoner answers by \emph{optimizing}: it descends a learned energy $E_\theta(h_x,z)$
over a latent and decodes the optimum. Energy-Based Transformers make this explicit and use the
scalar terminal energy as a confidence signal, thinking longer on hard inputs \citep{ebt2025}. But a
single scalar discards almost everything about the \emph{descent}. We ask whether the per-input
\emph{geometry} of that descent---how independent stochastic restarts agree, how sharp or flat the
optimum is, and how the dynamics settled---is a better, and \emph{certifiable}, signal of
correctness.

We run inference from $K$ stochastic restarts and read off basin agreement, local curvature of the
energy over the latent (via Hessian--vector products), and descent dynamics, then map this
$14$-feature descriptor to a nonconformity score. Wrapped in distribution-free risk control, this
yields two certificates: \emph{selective prediction} (answer only when a guaranteed error rate holds
among answered inputs, else abstain; via Learn-then-Test \citep{ltt2021}) and \emph{adaptive halting}
(stop as soon as a guaranteed error budget is met; via Conformal Risk Control \citep{crc2022}).

\paragraph{Contributions and what we falsify.} (1) The geometry features and the two
distribution-free certificates over an EBM reasoner. (2) A stated falsification criterion---if the
paired-bootstrap CI on $\Delta\mathrm{AURC}(\text{energy}-\text{geometry})$ includes zero, the core
claim fails---which we \emph{pass}: geometry beats the EBT scalar energy on two structurally distinct
tasks, and the lift is caused by the restart geometry (it grows with $K$; $K{=}1$ barely separates).
(3) An adversarial, negative result we report prominently: against a standard softmax-confidence
baseline, geometry only \emph{ties} (arithmetic) or \emph{loses} (graph). So the honest claim is that
inference-time landscape geometry beats the EBT scalar-energy signal specifically, while the
distribution-free certificate machinery---and its designed failure under shift---stands on its own.
