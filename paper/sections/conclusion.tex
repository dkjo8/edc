\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The geometry of an energy-based reasoner's inference-time descent is a usable reliability signal, and
distribution-free risk control turns it into two certificates---selective prediction and adaptive
halting---that are valid in-distribution and, by design, fail loudly under shift. Empirically,
landscape geometry beats the scalar terminal energy that Energy-Based Transformers use, the advantage
is caused by the restart geometry (basin agreement above all), and adaptive halting cuts inference
compute substantially at a guaranteed error budget.

\paragraph{Limitations, honestly.} Two matter. First, the guarantees are marginal under
exchangeability and provably void under distribution shift; we treat an abstention as a routing
signal, not a promise. Second, and more consequential: against a standard softmax-confidence baseline
(MSP, temperature scaling, entropy), geometry only ties (arithmetic) or loses (graph). The advantage
is over the EBT scalar-energy signal specifically, not over all confidences. We attribute this to the
reasoner---a supervised basin-center energy with a shallow decoder, whose softmax is already
informative---rather than to the certificate machinery, which is agnostic to which score is used.

\paragraph{Future work.} The clear next lever is a fully-learned, multi-basin landscape (IRED-style
annealed score matching) in place of the basin-center energy: a landscape where restart geometry can
carry signal a single-point softmax cannot. We scaffolded this direction but did not yet obtain a
working reasoner under it (the score network did not fit and a fixed-step sampler could not reach the
correct basin), leaving it as the priority experiment. Beyond that: scaling to language-scale EBTs,
richer geometry (full spectra, mode connectivity), and further tasks.
