\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The geometry of an energy-based reasoner's inference-time descent is a usable reliability signal, and
distribution-free risk control turns it into two certificates---selective prediction and adaptive
halting---that are valid in-distribution and, by design, fail loudly under shift. Empirically,
landscape geometry beats the scalar terminal energy that Energy-Based Transformers use, the advantage
is caused by the restart geometry (basin agreement above all), and adaptive halting cuts inference
compute substantially at a guaranteed error budget.

\paragraph{Limitations, honestly.} Two matter. First, the guarantees are marginal under
exchangeability and provably void under distribution shift; we treat an abstention as a routing
signal, not a promise. Second, the strength of the geometry signal depends on the landscape: under a
supervised basin-center reasoner, geometry beats scalar energy but only ties/loses to a well-%
calibrated softmax. That dependence is itself informative---and encouraging---because replacing the
landscape with a genuinely learned, multi-basin energy (per-class attractors trained by a
contrastive-plus-stationarity objective, annealed sampling) makes geometry beat softmax confidence
($4/5$ seeds), confirming that the simple landscape was the limiter, not the geometry idea.

\paragraph{Future work.} The learned-landscape result is on arithmetic and the margin over softmax is
small; widening it---larger models and richer landscapes (annealed score matching), the graph and
further tasks, more seeds---is the immediate next step. Beyond that: scaling to language-scale EBTs
and richer geometry (full spectra, mode connectivity).
